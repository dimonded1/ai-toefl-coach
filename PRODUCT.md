# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students preparing for TOEFL iBT who need a compact practice tool, a visible score target, daily tasks, and AI-assisted feedback. Typical context: self-study before an exam, short daily sessions, uneven skill levels across Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing, and Vocabulary.

## Product Purpose
AI TOEFL Coach helps a learner set a target score, complete practice tasks, see weak zones, get a predicted score, and generate a study plan from local progress data plus AI feedback. Success means the learner understands what to practice next and why.

## Brand Personality
Calm, practical, encouraging. The product should feel like a focused study dashboard, not a marketing page or a game-first interface.

## Anti-references
Avoid overloaded gamification, noisy emoji-heavy controls, decorative dashboards with unclear metrics, vague AI-coach copy, and interfaces where the learner cannot tell which action improves the score.

## Design Principles
- Make the next useful study action obvious on every screen.
- Treat score, weak zones, and daily plan as one connected learning loop.
- Keep the interface dense enough for repeated study, but avoid competing visual accents.
- Explain AI recommendations through evidence from mistakes, progress, and score gap.
- Prefer trustworthy exam-practice clarity over playful decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Assumption: target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support. The current code should be reviewed for emoji-only labels, focus states, and text contrast before production use.
